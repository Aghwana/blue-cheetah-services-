About us

Transportation you can depend on.

Blue Cheetah Services is a Nigerian transportation and logistics company focused on safe, reliable and professional mobility solutions.

We support companies and organizations that need dependable movement of employees, passengers and goods. Our approach combines practical transport experience, driver supervision, route management and operational monitoring.

Our Mission

To provide safe, reliable, affordable and professional transportation and logistics solutions while creating value for clients, partners and employees.


Our Vision

To become one of Nigeria’s most trusted transportation and logistics service providers.
